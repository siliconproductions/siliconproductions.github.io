This Privacy Policy describes how the Blower - Candle Blower Lite app ("the app", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. The app is published on Google Play by Silicon Productions.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account and does not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, or location. The following information is collected automatically by third-party software development kits (SDKs) included in the app:

  • Device or other identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) — collected by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads, and used for analytics and fraud prevention.
  • App interaction data (such as screens viewed, buttons tapped, feature usage, session statistics, and general device information like model and OS version) — collected by Google Firebase Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how the app is used and to improve it, and by Google AdMob for advertising measurement.

Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services, which process the data described above under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses data in AdMob
  • Google Firebase Analytics — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Microsoft Clarity (usage analytics) — privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your payment details.

Advertising and consent

The app shows ads (banner, interstitial, app-open, and optional rewarded ads) served by Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before personalized ads are served, and you can choose whether to consent. You can also reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Purchasing the one-time premium upgrade removes all advertising from the app.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Identifiers and interaction data are shared with the advertising and analytics providers listed above solely so that they can provide their services (ad serving, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention).

Data retention and deletion

The data described above is retained by the third-party providers according to their published retention policies. The app itself stores only local preferences on your device (for example, your selected sound, premium status, and whether you have rated the app); uninstalling the app deletes this local data. To request deletion of analytics data associated with your device identifiers, contact us at hello.siliconproductions@gmail.com and we will assist you.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad serving is provided by Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play's Families and Ads policies.

Security

All data transmitted by the app is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
